## The Story

You arrive into a new world, with nothing but the memory of the word *"Dawn"*. You must traverse the lands — and then the waters — to solve crises, run errands, and make some pretty women happy. You gather power, wisdom, and various friends along the way.

Raise your **World Resonance** by catching sight of even the rarest enemies — Archangel, the commander siren Wilhelmina, or the titanic humanoid shark Bob. Bob's real chill, though.

Collect all the shiny weapons — craft them, or fight for them. Grow your crops and make food and potions out of them. Collect gear, choose your stats and playstyle — and once you've figured it all out, come try your luck in the Arena.

But what is Dawn? Who is Dawn? You, the player? The advent of a new era? Or an unfathomable force from the depths of Hell?

## Systems

### 🌍 World & Exploration
A flat 2D grid, one tile per entity. Two surface types: land and water. The map is authored in [Tiled](https://www.mapeditor.org/) and processed through a custom Node.js conversion script. About 6,900 hand-placed tiles in Nuvatra.

### ⚔️ World Resonance
A single progression number that grows through quests, exploration, and time in the world. Common overworld enemies are defeated by comparing your WR against theirs — no combat required. First-kill bonuses reward discovery, not repetition. Passive accrual even when you're not playing.

> *Anti-grind by design. No build prison. No grind wall. Quest, explore, grow — that's the loop.*

### 🏟️ Arena Combat
Turn-based combat with a full stat sheet. Speed determines turn order. Abilities have elemental types, and your available elements are set by your equipped weapons. Evasion, Precision, and Critical systems add variance without removing agency. Every Arena NPC has a unique kit — Sylvie will poison you to death on turn one if you're not careful.

### 🌾 Farming
Three environments (Open Field, Greenhouse, Forest Floor), each unlockable via the tech tree. Crops have stage-based watering requirements and ideal seasons for bonus yields. **Grows offline** via timestamp-based simulation — your farm rewards time away from the game, not just time in it.

### 🗡️ Equipment & Relics
Weapons (swords, staves, bows, fans, spears, warhammers, axes, whips) and Relics. Items are **instanced** — each pickup is a unique object with its own `instanceId`. Set bonuses activate at 2/3/4 matching pieces. Passive gear effects — immunities, flat damage bonuses — layer on top of the core loop.

### 👥 NPCs & Quests
Per-NPC quest chains with multiple stages: `dialogue`, `give_item`, `kill`, `fight_npc`, `finish_location_event`, `watch_scene`, with composable `OR`/`AND` operators. NPCs move to new map positions as quests advance. Heart ranks grow through gifting. The whole system is JSON-driven — new characters and quests can be added without touching engine code.

### 🔨 Crafting & Economy
Alchemy, Smithing, Cooking, and Miscellaneous. Cooking costs **Time Points** — one earned per real minute of play. The Café sells items when you're out of ingredients. The Grocery handles miscellaneous goods.

### 🕰️ Time System
Two parallel clocks. Real-world time governs farming growth. In-game time advances one unit per step — 50 units per half-day (Dawnrise or Duskfall), 7 revoluts per season, four seasons per year.

## Tech Stack

| Tool | Role |
|------|------|
| **SvelteKit** | Frontend framework — all UI, routing, stores, services, components |
| **TypeScript** | Language — zero JavaScript in the source |
| **localStorage** | Save system — no server, no accounts. Survives indefinitely. Until you clear cache. |
| **Tiled** | Map design, region placement, NPC/enemy/event positioning |
| **Claude (Anthropic)** | AI coding assistant — architecture, systems, debugging, quest logic. Thinking partner. |
| **TensorArt** | AI image generation — all NPC artworks |
| **Affinity Designer** | Game logo, auxiliary UI, branding |
| **Veo 3 / WAN Model** | AI-generated character highlight videos |

## Running Locally

```bash
git clone https://github.com/<your-repo>/dawns-journey.git
cd dawns-journey
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:5173`. No environment variables, no database, no accounts. It just runs.

## A Note from the Developer

This is six months of work. Done during unemployment, during the specific discipline of making something because you need it to exist. The first web app I ever built that became something real. Started as an interactable birthday card for a friend. Ended up with a world, a story, a cast, a combat system, a farming system, a faction war, and a developer who is significantly better at this than when he started.

The save is `localStorage`. If the files exist, the game runs. That's the brief I gave myself, and I didn't compromise it.
